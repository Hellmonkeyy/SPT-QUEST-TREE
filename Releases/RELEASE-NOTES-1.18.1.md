# Quest Tracker 1.18.1

Install over 1.18.0 - replace both DLLs, keep your config.

## Copies you do not hold are charged

A build that fits two of the same part - two identical rails, say - priced both at zero if you
owned one loose copy, in the search, in the cost total and on the panel rows. Each copy past the
ones you actually hold is a purchase now: the gun's own preset parts count, the copy already on a
gun you own counts once, and every loose copy counts. A second copy nobody sells keeps its row and
says so - "the gun comes with 1; you have 1 loose - no trader but Fence sells another" - or names
the trader and loyalty level that does.

## Also

- The toolbar gains a third layout for panels narrower than about 1460 px: a shorter search box
  and "Mine (M)" in place of "My quests (M)", so the "N shown" count is readable at 1366 wide.
- The server refuses a zone harvest from a client newer than itself and skips a zone file stamped
  newer, with a reason in the log, instead of reading either as if current.
